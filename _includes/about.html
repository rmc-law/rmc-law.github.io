Before Cambridge, I worked at the Max Planck Institute for Psycholinguistics as a Research Project Coordinator. Previously, I studied for an MSc at the Donders Institute, worked as a predoctoral research assistant at NYU Abu Dhabi, studied for a BA at UCL Linguistics, and spent a summer at Harvard Psychology.
